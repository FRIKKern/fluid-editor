# Fluid editor · Sunday 22 November 2026

11 pull requests merged, one of them Saturday's News ([#755](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/755)). A check runs our editor through recorded steps and compares it with what Squarespace's real editor, the oracle, was seen to do. Eight pull requests went to the Scrolling block, whose words run along a wave: three to its rows, five to its link editor. Two went to how a short Quote moves the blocks around it.

## What a person can now do

Run `npm run dev` and open the editor.

### A Scrolling block's rows when the wave falls

**On desktop** ([#754](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/754)). Saturday's open gap.

1. Add a Scrolling block and raise its Wave Intensity to 120, then set it straight to 0.
2. The block falls to 1 row, as on the oracle, and the line may run over it. Before, it kept 2.
3. A fall of one step (30) keeps the rows that hold the line. A fall of two steps or more moves the block's foot up by the fall, snapped to the row, at least 1 row. The rule fits 124 oracle steps in six sessions.

**After the fall** ([#759](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/759)). Press Speed, Direction or Pause on hover. The rows stay, as in all 24 oracle reads. Before, 180 to 90 grew 3 rows to 4, and 120 to 0 grew 1 row to 2.

**In the phone view** ([#760](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/760)). Open Mobile View, then change the wave. The phone has its own rule, read in 34 steps: a rise takes the rows that hold the line with a small give, so a 60 line keeps 2 rows, and a fall of even one step moves the foot up, so 180 to 150 leaves 5 rows.

### The Scrolling block's link editor

1. In Content, press Attach link and pick a page, such as About.
2. **Backspace, arrows and letters stay in the field** ([#758](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/758)). Backspace takes one character. Before, it deleted the whole block, and the same in the Button, Image and Form link fields; arrows moved the block and Shift+S made it a Stack.
3. **Undo** ([#752](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/752)). Press Undo with the editor open: it closes and keeps the link. The next Undo takes the link.
4. **Escape** ([#757](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/757)). Escape, and Escape again, leave the editor open, its field on /about.
5. **Cmd+Z in the field** ([#762](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/762)). It takes the pick and leaves the editor open on /about; Attach link is an undo step of its own.
6. **Cmd+D, Cmd+Shift+H and arrows** ([#763](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/763)). From the field they no longer copy or hide the block, and the arrow keys on the editor's buttons no longer move it.

### A short Quote's next gesture

A group made narrower can leave a Quote short of its text, such as 0.42 rows ([#756](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/756), [#761](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/761)). On its next gesture, the Quote grows the rows its words need, rounded up. A block across its bottom line grows at least 1, and the other blocks take the overflow rounded, as for a Text. Before, a Shape across the line did not grow at all.

## Checks that got harder to fool

- scrolling-check grew from 115 to 228 rows over the day. Each PR's new rows were red on main's app, such as 11 of 146 for #754 and 13 of 208 for #760 ([#754](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/754), [#760](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/760), [#763](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/763)).
- mid-check, the check for what blocks show mid-drag, went from 1,341 of 1,347 to 1,350 of 1,356, the 6 misses being the known ones ([#761](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/761)).
- Two of our own rows that had never been read were found wrong on the oracle and changed: what Undo does after Edit reopens a link, and an arrow key after a pick ([#762](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/762), [#763](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/763)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- None of the five read rules is claimed as a fresh prediction: each guess was kept only in a local file, or the rule was fitted after the reads (#754, #756, #759, #760, #761).
- Not read on the oracle: a drag or arrow keys through the wave steps, a block made taller by hand, and Fade edges, Item Spacing, Text Style and Text Size after a fall, which still refit the rows ([#754](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/754), [#759](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/759)). In the phone view, only one block per section was read ([#760](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/760)).
- With nothing picked, Escape twice leaves the oracle's link editor open; ours closes it ([#762](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/762)). While the editor is open, the oracle's Link row still reads Attach link; ours shows the page at once ([#757](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/757)).
- The link field's key fix was not read on the oracle; a field taking its own keys is the browser's default ([#758](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/758)).
