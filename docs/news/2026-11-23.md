# Fluid editor · Monday 23 November 2026

8 pull requests merged. A check runs our editor through recorded steps and compares it with what Squarespace's real editor, the oracle, was seen to do. Five went to the Scrolling block, whose words run along a wave, two to how a short Quote moves the blocks around it, and one stopped two checks crashing when run one after another.

## What a person can now do

Run `npm run dev` and open the editor.

### A Scrolling block keeps its rows

**Fade edges and Item Spacing** ([#770](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/770)).

1. Add a Scrolling block, raise its Wave Intensity to 180, then set it to 90.
2. Press Fade edges, or Item Spacing on its range. The rows stay, as on the oracle. Before, they refit to 4 rows.
3. Pull the block's bottom handle down 2 rows, then press Fade edges. The block keeps its new height. Before, it shrank back.
4. Text Size still refits to the new line, as on the oracle.

**Phone drags** ([#766](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/766)). In Mobile View, dragging the Wave Intensity range from 180 to 90 leaves 3 phone rows, where a press leaves 4. Ours already did this; nine new rows pin it.

### The link editor

- **Escape with nothing picked** ([#764](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/764)). Press Attach link and do not pick a page. The first Escape takes the page list, and every Escape after leaves the editor open, its empty field focused. Cmd+Z then takes Attach link's own step. Before, the second Escape closed the editor.
- **Chrome's own shortcuts stay off** ([#767](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/767), [#771](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/771)). Since Sunday's [#763](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/763), Cmd+Shift+H in a link field opened the home page in the tab and left the editor, and plain Cmd+D opened Chrome's bookmark dialog. Both are cancelled again, with Cmd+Shift+D; the block is still not hidden or copied.

### A short Quote's next gesture

A group made narrower can leave a Quote short of its text ([#765](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/765), [#768](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/768)). Drag such a Quote down 2 rows. A block above its bottom line that starts below the Quote's top now moves by where its middle sits, as if the overflow stretched the Quote's rows evenly. At 2.57 rows short, a Shape on rows 4 to 6 moves 2, and one on rows 3 to 5 moves 1. A Line and a Shape in the same place do the same: the kind does not matter.

- For the Quote rules, predictions were pushed before each capture, and #768's middle-line rule was read exactly as pushed on its two test cases ([#768](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/768)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- Sunday's link-field change broke two browser shortcuts for a day: Cmd+Shift+H left the editor and Cmd+D opened the bookmark dialog. The first fix left Cmd+D out; a second PR caught it ([#767](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/767), [#771](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/771)).
- Not read on the oracle: Item Spacing by a drag or its number box, Cmd+Z after these presses, and the phone view after a fall ([#770](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/770)); a link typed, then Escape ([#764](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/764)); a Quote whose top is not on row 1 for the middle-line rule ([#768](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/768)).
- No guess was written down before #770's reads ([#770](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/770)).
- text-check's own crash was not reproduced, and its full run takes over 25 minutes locally ([#769](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/769)).
