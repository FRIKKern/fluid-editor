# Fluid editor · Friday 27 November 2026

19 pull requests merged, one of them the News for Thursday ([#812](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/812)). A check runs our editor through recorded steps and compares it with what Squarespace's real editor, the oracle, was seen to do. Thirteen PRs changed how the Scrolling block, whose words run along a wave, answers a Wave Intensity drag and its keys and moves the blocks around it, two added guard rows where ours already matched, and three made checks steadier.

## What a person can now do

Run `npm run dev` and open the editor. An area such as 3/2/5/10 reads top row, left column, bottom row and right column of the section's grid. The foot is the Scrolling block's bottom edge.

### Holding a Wave Intensity drag

1. In Mobile View, add a Scrolling block and press 90. Drag Wave Intensity 90>120>150>180 and back to 90, and hold. The block shows 1/2/4/10, the rows a press to 90 gives. Before, it showed 1/2/5/10 until the let go ([#809](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/809)).
2. Drag 90>120>150 and press Cmd+Z while still holding. The drag so far is taken back: the value is 90, Undo and Redo are on, and letting go takes no step. Before, Cmd+Z undid the press to 90, and the range then brought 150 back as a fresh step ([#810](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/810)). The range keeps the focus through it ([#822](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/822)).
3. A tap or a blur that makes the range fire a change mid-drag no longer takes a step or stops the drag: the value still follows the pointer, and the let go is one step ([#814](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/814)). With the focus then on the page and the block's panel open, an arrow key moves nothing. Before, it moved the block a column, 1/2/5/26 to 1/3/5/27 ([#824](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/824)).
4. On the desktop, step Wave Intensity with the arrow keys. The range keeps the focus through Cmd+Z and Cmd+Shift+Z, Cmd+Z ends a run of keys so the next key is a step of its own, and Right then Left back to the start is one step ([#818](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/818)).

### The blocks around the foot, on the desktop

- **A growth moves every block below down**, by a drag or a press ([#811](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/811)). Thursday's rule that a drag's growth moves only a block the new foot reaches is gone: a new read found the oracle has no such rule ([#821](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/821)).
- **A fall leaves a block above the old foot where it is while it starts on or above the new foot**, by a press or a drag; other blocks follow the foot up ([#821](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/821), [#823](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/823)).
- **A fall to 0** lands a block on the foot, and the blocks after it go up by that block's move. A Text block at 12/2/14/10 now ends at 9/2/11/10, 3 rows up, as on the oracle; ours gave 7/2/9/10 ([#827](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/827)).
- **A shorter block beside the Scrolling block stays.** A block on its top row and columns that ends above its foot is no longer stretched as the Scrolling block grows ([#813](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/813)).
- **Rows made by hand are kept.** A Scrolling block made taller by hand keeps its rows on a growth while its line fits in them: at 1/2/4/26 it stays so at 30 and 60, and takes 1/2/5/26 at 90 ([#820](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/820)). A fall of two steps or more keeps the rows while the block has no more than the new line needs: 150, 60, 0 now stays at 1/2/3/26, where ours gave 1/2/2/26. Text Size falls take the same rules ([#826](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/826)).
- **The phone stack follows the desktop as it was before a step**, also on each live step of a drag ([#815](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/815)).

## Checks that got harder to fool, and steadier

- scrolling-check grew from 898 to 1,357 rows. Each behaviour change came with rows red on the old app. Two PRs added guard rows only, where ours already matched the oracle ([#816](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/816), [#822](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/822)).
- A drag in scrolling-check is now one call to the browser. Each move had been its own call, and under load the gaps passed the 1000 ms pause that makes a step of its own: one row went red about 1 run in 3. It is now green in 10 of 10 runs ([#819](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/819)).
- row-check's frames reads start their 600 ms at the move itself. With a 1.1 s gap planted before each held move, main passed 14 of 22 row-check rows; with the fix, every frames read passes, 13 of 13 ([#825](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/825)).
- Twice on Friday a hosted browser-checks job went red before any check ran: a third-party package source answered 403 during Chrome's setup. Setup on Linux now sets such sources aside, and all 43 jobs ran green ([#817](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/817)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- The phone view was not read for the desktop fall rules; on a fall to 0 it keeps the old per-block rule ([#821](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/821), [#823](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/823), [#827](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/827)).
- After a block was made taller by hand, the oracle's Cmd+Z gives back other areas than ours, so those Cmd+Z rows are left out ([#813](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/813), [#820](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/820), [#826](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/826)). It is filed apart.
- A held touch drag, and a tap during one, were not read ([#809](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/809), [#814](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/814)). ArrowUp and ArrowDown with the panel open were built by inference ([#824](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/824)).
- Two blocks that would land on the foot from different rows were not read; ours takes the smaller move up ([#827](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/827)).
