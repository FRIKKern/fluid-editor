# Fluid editor · Thursday 26 November 2026

9 pull requests merged, one of them the News for Wednesday ([#806](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/806)). A check runs our editor through recorded steps and compares it with what Squarespace's real editor, the oracle, was seen to do. Every other PR went to the Scrolling block, whose words run along a wave: five to how its Wave Intensity drag and undo move blocks, three to scrolling-check, the check that reads it.

## What a person can now do

Run `npm run dev` and open the editor. An area such as 3/2/5/10 reads top row, left column, bottom row and right column of the section's grid.

### Ending a Wave Intensity drag

1. Open Mobile View, add a Scrolling block with a Text block under it, and press 90. The Scrolling block is at 1/2/5/10.
2. Drag Wave Intensity out to 180 and back to 90, and press Escape while the pointer is still held. The selection goes, the panel closes, the block lands at 1/2/4/10 and the section at 5 rows, as when the drag is let go. The first Cmd+Z gives back 1/2/5/10, the next the section as built. Before, the block stayed at 1/2/5/10 and the first Cmd+Z went straight back to the start ([#802](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/802)).
3. On the desktop, drag out and let go back on 90. The range keeps the focus, so one ArrowRight steps Wave Intensity to 120. Before, ArrowRight moved the block one column, to 1/3/5/27 ([#803](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/803)).
4. A second finger or pointer lifted during the drag no longer ends it: only the pointer that holds the drag lets it go ([#808](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/808)).

### Rows after a drag or an undo

- On the desktop, with the Text block a row under the foot, drag 90 out to 180, back to 90 and out to 180 again. The Text block ends at 8/2/10/10 in a 12-row section, as on the oracle. Ours took it down by every growth, to 10/2/12/10 and 14 rows. A growth now takes down only a block the new foot reaches ([#800](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/800)).
- In the phone view, press 180, 150, 120, let go and press Cmd+Z. 150 comes back at 1/2/7/10, the Text block at 7/2/9/10, the section 8 rows: the rows a growth to 150 takes. Ours gave back the rows the fall had left: a 7-row section. A Cmd+Z that takes the wave down still keeps the stored rows ([#801](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/801)).

## Checks that got harder to fool

- scrolling-check grew from 820 to 898 rows. Each behaviour change came with rows red on the old app.
- A new follow guard reaches a hand move while a Wave foot move is pending, through the phone Cmd+Z refit above. On a planted break it is the only red row ([#804](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/804)).
- Ten phone plans now also check their last read, back in Desktop View after the last Cmd+Z. Each of three planted breaks turns all or most of them red ([#805](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/805)).
- The Escape row now holds each block's selection and the panel too. A break that kept the block selected passed before ([#807](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/807)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- The second-pointer rows were not read on the oracle, and no real two-finger touch screen was used ([#808](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/808)). The touch version of the focus fix was not read on the oracle either ([#803](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/803)).
- Just before the Escape, while the drag is still held, the oracle shows 1/2/4/10 and ours 1/2/5/10. It is filed apart ([#802](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/802)).
- A press growth that does not reach a block below still moves it here; that was not read. Nor were the states during a drag, or the desktop drag rule in the phone view ([#800](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/800)).
- The phone Cmd+Z refit was not read on the desktop, after a two-step fall, or for Redo ([#801](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/801)). Escape during a desktop drag or a touch drag was not read ([#802](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/802)).
