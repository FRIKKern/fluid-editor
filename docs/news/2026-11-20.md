# Fluid editor · Friday 20 November 2026

13 pull requests merged, one of them Thursday's News ([#736](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/736)). A check runs our editor through recorded steps and compares it with what Squarespace's real editor, the oracle, was seen to do. Three pull requests made the Scrolling block work in new ways, and a fourth stored a check's list again after them. Three changed how a widened Text lets go of its rows. Five made a check harder to fool or stopped one failing a correct editor.

## What a person can now do

Run `npm run dev` and open the editor.

### The Scrolling block

The Scrolling block runs a line of words along a wave. Each part below was read on the oracle, and scrolling-check, the Scrolling block's own check, grew from 27 to 87 cases over the day, all passing on each branch.

**The Content list** ([#742](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/742)). Select a Scrolling block and open its Content list. Before, it did nothing. Read twice on the oracle, with a real mouse and keys:

1. Press Add Item. "Dream it" is added at the end, and the panel stays on the list.
2. Press a row. Its view opens with Back, Text, Duplicate and Delete.
3. Type in Text. The block shows each key as you type.
4. Press Duplicate to put a copy after the item, or Delete to remove it. Both go back to the list.
5. On the list, a row's trash deletes it at once. The last item has no trash.
6. Drag a row by its grip. While you hold it, the rows move and the block stays. Let go, and the block follows.

Each change, the typing included, is one undo step. On main's app the new check passed 29 of 45; on the branch, 45 of 45.

**Words along the wave** ([#744](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/744)). The words now move along the wave in the editor, on `page.html` and in the export. They move at 60 px a second times Speed, to the left or right as Direction says. Wave Intensity sets how deep the wave dips, Item Spacing sets the gap between words, and Fade edges fades the outer 15% at each side. Pause on hover does not pause in the editor, as on the oracle (two reads). A block with text and no items, such as a heading on the imported Home page, draws its text along the wave. On main's app all 34 new rows failed; on the branch, 79 of 79 passed.

**Attach link** ([#745](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/745)). Press Attach link in the block's panel. The link editor opens beside the panel, level with the button. Pick a page, and the whole block becomes the link, opening in the same tab, or a new one if you set new tab. The Link row then shows the page, its path, Edit, Copy and Remove Link. Remove Link takes the link off, and Attach link comes back. In the editor, a press on the linked block selects it and does not follow the link. On main's app the 7 new rows failed; on the branch, 87 of 87 passed.

coverage-check keeps a stored list of the editor's controls, and which of ours are drawn but do nothing yet. After #742 it failed on main for about four and a half hours, since its list still named Add Item and Attach link as doing nothing, on desktop and on phone. [#747](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/747) stored the list again: those 4 rows left it, its count went from 91 to 87, and the 541 that match stayed.

### A widened Text lets go as the oracle does

On a desktop-width window, since Thursday a long Text block widened in one drag holds the rows it had at the drop until you let it go with Escape. In the oracle read behind all three changes, a long Text widened to grid line 15 at 1600 px wide holds 12 rows; when the hold falls, it has 10. Each change was read twice on the oracle, the reads agreeing, and adds mid-check rows that fail on the app before it.

**Type into a held Text** ([#737](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/737)). Double-click the held Text to edit it. The hold falls as the edit opens, and the block has 10 rows. Paste four short paragraphs and let go: the block has 13 rows, as on the oracle, and keeps the rows its text needs. Read twice, 14 of 14 reads alike. Of 8 new rows, main's app failed 4.

**Switch view, Undo and Redo** ([#740](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/740)). Switch to Mobile View while the Text is held: the hold falls, and the desktop block has 10 rows, also after you go back to desktop. Press Cmd+Z, and the widen is undone. Press Cmd+Shift+Z, and the block is back at 12 rows, held again; Escape then takes it to 10. Read twice, 11 of 11 alike. Of 7 new rows, the app before failed 4.

**A view change between Undo and Redo** ([#743](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/743)). This merged later the same day and narrows #740's Redo. If the view changes at any time after the Undo, even to Mobile View and back, Redo brings the block back at 12 rows and it stays at 12: Escape no longer takes it to 10, as on the oracle. Redo with no view change still falls on Escape, as in #740. A review of #740 found the gap. Of 8 new rows, main's app failed 2.

## Checks that got harder to fool

Each fix below is proved on a scratch build with a fault forced on purpose: the old check passes it, the new one fails it. #741 runs the other way: it stops a correct editor from failing.

**Gate C, the replay of recorded oracle gestures** ([#734](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/734)). Since Thursday, a block left in another section is a diff. An extra section with no blocks added nothing to the diff. Now every section other than the gesture's own is a diff. On a build that leaves an empty section, the old replay passed 2 of 2 and the new one failed both. Unforced, Gate C passes 23 of 23, with 23 of 23 undos.

**Thursday's desktop and phone reads** ([#735](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/735)). mid-check, the check for what blocks show while a drag or resize is held, had no rows for the oracle reads behind Thursday's change: desktop and phone treat a widened Text differently. 18 new rows replay them. A build that drops the phone's one-line test now fails 2 phone rows, and the editor from before Thursday's change fails 4 desktop rows. The stored run was 1,295 of 1,301, with the same 6 named known misses.

**Float's quick press** ([#739](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/739)). anim-check, the animation check, has a row for the play that runs when Float's editor opens. Its quick click is timed from our click. A slow click, up to the old 500 ms limit between the button going down and the click, let a wrong timing pass: with a 450 ms stall, a play that waits for 1,000 ms after the button goes down matched. Now a quick press with more than 50 ms between down and click is pressed again on a fresh block, up to 3 tries; if the last is still over 50 ms, the row fails as "press gap". Unforced, that gap is 1 to 3 ms, so a right app is normally not pressed again. The row's name and total are unchanged.

**Outline fades** ([#738](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/738)). marks-check is the check for small marks the editor draws, such as hover outlines, handles and the grid. Its outline fade rows read only the top border's colour, and tested points from the outline's own box. So a 6 by 6 px dot in a corner, or an outline lit only on top, passed. Now a fade frame fails if its four sides are not one colour, or if the outline's box is more than 2 px off its section's. Plain fades and real stalls still pass, also at 4 times slower CPU. No row was added or loosened.

**A stage on a half pixel** ([#741](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/741)). This one stops a correct editor from failing. The outline rows test 4 points on the outline's border. A point in the window's last half pixel tests the pixel past it and finds nothing, so with the stage, the frame that holds the page, on a half pixel a correct outline read "under nothing" on every frame. Points now stay a pixel inside the view's right and bottom edges, still on the 3 px border. A half-pixel build went from 8 of 12 to 12 of 12. It had not shown up yet: today the stage sits on whole pixels. Every forced hidden-outline case from #725 and #738 still fails.

## Where it is slow or rough

- Attach link differs from one oracle read in three ways. After Escape, the oracle's link field still showed the page; ours closes the editor. One Undo after that left the oracle's link in place with Redo off; ours undoes the pick. The oracle's Link row showed the page only after that press; ours shows it at once ([#745](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/745)).
- Some Scrolling behaviour is inferred, not read: the page pick as one undo step, Remove Link, Pause on hover with a link in Preview ([#745](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/745)), and Pause on hover on a published page, which needs a Save to read ([#744](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/744)).
- The oracle's Scrolling block snaps to grid rows round a taller wave and centres the line in it; ours does not yet ([#744](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/744)).
- How a narrowed Text's hold lets go was not read on the oracle; it stays as it was ([#737](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/737), [#740](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/740)).
- mid-check still names 6 known misses. The last stored run is 1,310 of 1,316 ([#740](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/740)); #743's 8 rows are not in it, and its full local run was still going when it was written ([#743](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/743)).
- coverage-check failed on main for about four and a half hours, from #742 until its list was stored again ([#747](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/747)).
- The full anim-check and marks-check were not run locally for #738, #739 and #741; they were left to the hosted browser checks ([#738](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/738), [#739](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/739), [#741](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/741)).
