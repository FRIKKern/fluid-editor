# Fluid editor · Wednesday 18 November 2026

18 pull requests merged, one of them Tuesday's News ([#705](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/705)). A check runs our editor through recorded steps and compares it with what Squarespace's real editor, the oracle, was seen to do. Wednesday started a new trial: a fresh Squarespace trial site became the oracle. Four pull requests set it up and wrote down how. Four changed the editor itself, in small ways, each to match the oracle (one of them is also a trial pull request). Ten made a check harder to fool.

Several reads below were "read twice": the same steps were recorded two separate times on the new trial, and the change rests on both recordings.

## What a person can now do

Run `npm run dev` and open the editor.

**Strike at a caret** ([#711](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/711)). In a text block, put the caret after a word and press Strike in the text bar. The button now shows as on at once; before, it showed off until you typed a letter. Type, and the letters are struck. Press Strike again and it shows off. Read twice on the oracle (16 reads each, all agreeing). Bold and Italic already matched.

**Float's opening play** ([#710](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/710)). Opening an animation preset's editor with its tile's Edit plays the animation once. Read twice with the press held down (1.5 s and 0.8 s), the oracle starts that play from the click, not from the moment the button goes down: its first move came 777 and 817 ms after the click. Ours started 170 ms after the click and first moved about 507 ms after it. It now starts 470 ms after the click and first moves about 806 ms after.

**The font list** ([#706](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/706), [#708](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/708)). The new trial's list has 1,504 names, one more than the old one: Adelle Sans, after Adamina. Ours has it too. Google Fonts does not serve it, as with 15 other names, so its row and value work but the page falls back to another font. The oracle's list also scrolls 33 px past its last row (read on the old site); ours stopped at the last row and now has the same 33 px.

## The new trial

The checks compare against reads of the oracle stored in the repo: its footer, its font lists, its theme colours. A new trial means a new oracle site, so those reads were checked against it ([#706](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/706)). A read-back found 10 differences, all small:

- 4 in the footer: the Instagram link has a different label, since no Instagram account is linked on the new trial.
- 4 font lists whose first screen now shows Adelle Sans, which pushes Alike Angular off the end.
- The font list's count (1,504, was 1,503) and one name early in the list (Adelle Sans, was ADLaM Display).

The new reads were stored, the checks now use the new site by default, and a second read-back found no differences.

`QUALITY.md`, "A new trial", now holds the steps so an agent can set up the next one ([#707](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/707)): open the account in the browser that is already signed in (a sign-in page means stop and ask), copy that login into the oracle's own Chrome, open the template and press START WITH THIS DESIGN, set the six font rows to Poppins and save, then add the two probe pages, the pages the checks work on. These are Wednesday's steps, written down; they were not run again, since that would start another trial.

The copied login lasts only while that Chrome runs. A fresh start comes up signed out and must copy the login again. The steps and the script's own messages now say so ([#712](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/712), [#721](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/721)).

## Checks that got harder to fool

Most prove their point the same way: force a fault on purpose in a scratch copy, then show the old check passes it and the new one fails. [#722](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/722) runs the other way: it stops a correct editor from failing.

**Save after an Undo** ([#709](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/709)). Monday's page named one real gap: add a Text block, write, press Undo, and our Save turned off while the oracle's stayed on. Read twice on the new trial, the oracle turns Save off too once Undo brings the page back to how it loaded. Its Save had stayed on in the old recording only because the steps began by adding a blank section, which was still there. Our replay skipped that step. It now adds the blank section, the known miss is gone, and text-check, the check for typing and undo, compares two more recordings: 1,069 of 1,069 rows, up from 1,025. With #711's reads it is 1,101.

**The Float row** ([#713](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/713), [#716](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/716), [#718](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/718), [#722](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/722)). After #710 the row must tell a play timed from the click from one timed from the button going down. Its press put the two 1 ms apart, so it could not. It now presses three times: held 0.8 s, held 1.5 s, and a quick click, each against the oracle's read with that press. An app that times its play from the button going down, or waits for the later of the two, fails. The quick click is now one step, so load on the machine no longer stretches the gap between down and up (it was seen up to 2.9 s); a gap over 500 ms fails as "press gap". anim-check, the animation check, passes 408 of 408, with no known misses (before #710: 407 and one known miss).

**Outline fades** ([#714](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/714), [#715](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/715), [#717](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/717)). Following Tuesday's work on the 0.2 s outline fade, three more broken fades now fail: one that starts 100 to 150 ms late while frames keep coming, one whose outline is taken off the page after a fade frame, and one whose outline is still there but hidden, boxless or see-through. A real stall, where no frames come, still passes.

**Shapes' Spread** ([#719](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/719)). Shuffle could set Shapes' Spread row (Linear, Edge, Center) any way at all, and the check only printed a note. Read twice on the oracle, 33 Shuffle presses each: every press picks one of the three, at even odds. A new row reads 40 Shuffle presses; each press must land on a button the oracle showed, and each of the three must come up.

**A closed panel stays closed** ([#720](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/720)). After Escape closes the panel, the row selected the block again by code and read the panel once, 150 ms later. Now it selects the block with a real pointer press, as on the oracle, and watches the panel for 800 ms. A panel that comes back late, or only on a real press, fails.

## Where it is slow or rough

- The old site's full font list read was not taken again on the new trial. Its counts predate Adelle Sans, and the 33 px under the last row comes from that one read ([#706](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/706), [#708](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/708)).
- The demo page was not rebuilt on the new trial; that is the owner's call ([#706](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/706)).
- The live read-back takes over 10 minutes and prints only at the end; a 580 s timeout killed it with no output ([#712](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/712)).
- One criterion of the text-bar work was changed to what the oracle showed (Bold reads off with the caret just before a bold word) and still needs a second pair of eyes ([#711](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/711)).
- A panel that reopens more than 800 ms after the press is still not caught ([#720](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/720)).
- On Linux CI, marks-check still names 5 font rows as known misses: 1,724 of 1,729 ([#714](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/714)).
