# Fluid editor · Saturday 21 November 2026

6 pull requests merged, one of them Friday's News ([#748](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/748)). A check runs our editor through recorded steps and compares it with what Squarespace's real editor, the oracle, was seen to do. Three pull requests changed the editor: one in the Scrolling block, two in how Undo and Redo work inside a Text's edit. Two made a check harder to fool.

## What a person can now do

Run `npm run dev` and open the editor.

### The Scrolling block's line sits in the middle

The Scrolling block runs a line of words along a wave ([#749](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/749)). On Friday the oracle was seen to centre that line in the block's rows, and ours did not yet.

1. Add a Scrolling block.
2. Raise its Wave Intensity, for example to 90.
3. The block grows to 4 rows and the line sits in their middle, 14.7 from the top, as on the oracle. Before, any wave that was not flat drew the line at the top of the block.

At 0, 30, 90 and 180 the line's top and height now match the oracle's reads exactly (for example 18.6 and 34 at 0; 10.8 and 214 at 180 with 6 rows).

### Undo and Redo inside a Text's edit

Both were read on the oracle in two sessions that agreed, 15 of 15 reads each.

**A widened Text's hold comes back** ([#746](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/746)). Since Friday, on desktop, a long Text widened in one drag holds its rows from the drop until let go.

1. Widen a long Text in one drag, so it holds 12 rows.
2. Press Cmd+Z, double-click the Text to edit it, and press Cmd+Shift+Z inside the edit.
3. The hold comes back. Close the edit and let go: the Text drops to 10 rows, as on the oracle. Before, it kept all 12.

Cmd+Z inside a Text's edit now also takes back any block's live hold, as Undo outside an edit does, and typing in the edited Text drops that block's hold.

**A narrowing undone inside the edit** ([#751](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/751)).

1. Narrow a Text by 2 columns, then press Cmd+Z.
2. Double-click it, press Cmd+Shift+Z inside the edit, then Cmd+Z there.
3. The Text is back as it was before the narrowing, with its 12 rows. Before, it stayed narrow, at 9 rows, too short for its text.

## Checks that got harder to fool

- **scrolling-check wants the words moving outside the editor** ([#750](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/750)). It now reads the words' speed, 60 px/s, in Preview, on the saved page and in the exported page, with the mouse off the block, and that Pause on hover stops and starts them. With the words forced to stand still outside the editor, the old check passed and the new one fails 9 rows. scrolling-check grew from 87 to 115 cases over the day ([#749](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/749), [#750](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/750)).
- **mid-check's cross-block row checks its own steps** ([#753](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/753)). mid-check is the check for what blocks show mid-drag. The row added by #746 for a hold redone in another Text's edit now fails if its drag missed or its double-click left the edit shut; before, a missed step passed. At the day's last merge mid-check passed 1,332 of 1,338, the 6 misses being the known ones.

## Where it is slow or rough

- A Scrolling block taken straight from a wave of 120 to 0 falls to 1 row on the oracle and its line overflows the block; ours does not do this yet, and the rule behind it was not found ([#749](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/749)).
- Four of #746's new rows want what follows from the oracle reads, not what was read on it ([#746](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/746)).
- The narrowing undone in an edit was not read on the phone, or with another block below the Text ([#751](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/751)).
- #753's guard was proved with a missed drag only; a missed double-click was not forced ([#753](https://github.com/FRIKKern/fluid-editor/pull/753)).
